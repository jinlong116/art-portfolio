Artist Statement

"Painting is an act of listening to the quiet spaces between light, matter, and memory."

Born in Zurich and currently working between Paris and Berlin, Elena Vance’s practice centers on the physical dynamics of pigment and organic surfaces. Her oil compositions investigate how light fractures through multi-layered glazes, evoking landscapes that exist at the boundary of tangible geography and meditative abstraction.

Working primarily on fine Belgian linen and hand-prepared cotton rag, Vance applies pigments, cold wax, and charcoal in slow, contemplative strata. Each painting undergoes multiple cycles of addition and reductive scraping, leaving behind visible traces of time and process.

Her works are included in notable private and museum collections across Europe, North America, and East Asia.
